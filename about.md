I'm Samuel, a postgraduate cybersecurity student at the University of Sunderland with a background spanning networking, cloud, and security engineering.

## Background

I hold a **CCNA** and an **AWS Solutions Architect** certification, alongside prior professional experience as a network engineering technician. My postgraduate studies focus on applied cybersecurity — including offensive security techniques, network defence, and secure system design — with hands-on lab work in Kali Linux, Wireshark, and Cisco Packet Tracer.

## What I work on

My technical interests sit at the intersection of three areas:

- **Networking** — enterprise network design, routing and switching, VLANs, VPN and site-to-site connectivity, and secure network architecture.
- **Cybersecurity** — vulnerability assessment, penetration testing methodology, network defence, and security tooling.
- **Cloud** — AWS architecture with a focus on secure, scalable, multi-tier deployments and infrastructure as code.

The projects on this site reflect that mix. Some are drawn from academic coursework; others are self-directed builds designed to demonstrate specific technical skills.
